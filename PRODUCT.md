# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary A — Hiring Managers / Recruiters (full-time):** HR generalists and department heads at mid-to-large Vietnamese and regional companies. They scan dozens of profiles daily; they need to immediately see *results* and *credibility*, not just a list of responsibilities. Typically view on desktop in an office context.

**Primary B — Marketing Directors / Founders (freelance):** Decision-makers vetting a consultant for a campaign or e-commerce project. They care about proven revenue outcomes (VND GMV, growth multiples) and cross-industry range. Often referred through LinkedIn; they arrive with high intent but low patience.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that converts a viewer into a contact in under 90 seconds. Success = reader scrolls to the contact section and sends a message or schedules a call. Every design decision should reduce friction toward that action.

## Brand Personality

**Decisive · Evidenced · Distinctive**

Voice: direct and confident, never boastful. The numbers speak; the design frames them. A slight edge of bold character that signals Cuong is not just competent but interesting to work with.

## Anti-references

- Avoid: *too flashy / visually chaotic* — excessive animation layers, neon-overload, hard-to-read contrast ratios, elements competing for attention simultaneously.
- Avoid: generic white-blue LinkedIn-template aesthetics — no beige/sand/cream body, no "Modern Resume" Canva energy.
- Avoid: AI-scaffold patterns — eyebrows on every section, identical card grids with no hierarchy, same-size everything.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Every case study leads with a measurable outcome. Design makes numbers the hero, not decoration around them.
2. **Earn the boldness.** The dark palette and magenta accent are deliberate; use them at full commitment or not at all. No apologetic half-measures.
3. **Motion serves clarity.** Animations reveal information hierarchy; they don't perform. One entrance per section, not a cascade of independent triggers.
4. **Credibility over cleverness.** If a design choice makes the portfolio look "portfolio-y" without reinforcing Cuong's story, cut it.
5. **Mobile-complete.** Hiring managers forward links on mobile; the experience must be uncompromised at 375px, not just functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large/bold text).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must be respected — all animations should have a crossfade/instant fallback.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile.
- No information conveyed by color alone.
