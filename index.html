01 Community Impact & Research

Philippines Community Wellness Campaign

BYSM Bless The Children Foundation  •  Tondo, Manila  •  09–11/2025
Field Research Workshop Design Community Impact
The Challenge / Problem

Women and children in the Tondo district of Manila were facing severe psychological stress, but no one had clear data on what the actual pain points were. BYSM needed field research to identify the real problem before designing any intervention program.

What I Did
  • Went to the field in Tondo on Day 1 and directly surveyed the community; analyzed all BYSM data plus new survey data across Days 2–3
  • Wrote a research report identifying the core pain point: lack of practical tools for emotional regulation and family communication — not lack of awareness
  • Designed 6 workshops split across 2 target groups (mothers and children) based directly on research insights
  • Each workshop ran twice (morning and afternoon) to maximize community reach; facilitated all 6 sessions using the “I-Feel” emotional regulation technique
My Role & Impact

End-to-end ownership: survey design, field data collection, data analysis, insight report writing, workshop design, and facilitation of all 6 sessions.

05 Social Business & Research

Fridggie Vietnam

Social Business Creation 2024 (SBC Global)  •  SBC Global Competition 2024
Consumer Research Market Segmentation Go-to-Market ESG Integration
The Challenge / Problem

Vietnam ranks 2nd in Asia-Pacific for food waste — 8.8 million tons discarded annually, equal to 2% of GDP. Existing apps targeted supply chain issues and missed the real driver: unmanaged household behavior. The challenge was to prove this with real data, then build a profitable business model that still solves an urgent environmental problem.

What I Did
  • Led primary research across Hanoi, Da Nang, and HCMC; conducted 19 in-depth interviews (25–40 min each) and distributed surveys to 419 respondents to map consumer fridge-management personas
  • Built customer personas and market segmentation across 4 groups; mapped competitive landscape across all iOS and Android food apps and identified key feature gaps
  • Ran quantitative research in Round 2 across 3 regions with 240 respondents, testing app concept, social mantra, and purchase intent
  • Authored the full academic case study covering business model validation, ESG integration, financial projections, and crowdfunding; engineered a freemium go-to-market subscription app concept integrated with Bach Hoa Xanh, Aeon, and Go!
My Role & Impact

Research methodology design, multi-city fieldwork execution, quantitative/qualitative analysis, segmentation mapping, academic case study writing, go-to-market strategy, and international pitch support.
